OUR MECHANICAL LEGACY

Engineering Precision, Serving Trust Since 2011

At AutoCare, we operate on a single core belief: luxury cars require meticulous, uncompromising mechanical standards. Founded in 2011 by master tuning engineers, we grew from a boutique classic car garage into a premier multi-bay automotive facility equipped with factory-grade diagnostic telemetry systems.

Every vehicle entrusted to us undergoes rigorous checks. We analyze raw data logs, verify tolerances down to microscopic levels, and only deploy OEM-certified components. Whether it is a twin-turbo engine rebuild, a custom exhaust tuning, or precision laser wheel alignments, we deliver flawless performance that respects your investment.
