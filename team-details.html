Chief Master Mechanic (15+ Years Experience)

Marcus Vance coordinates shop operations at AutoCare. With an elite background in performance racing powertrains and high-voltage electrical setups, he bridges old-school mechanics with future-proof hybrid diagnostics.

Biography

Marcus began his journey with mechanical repair systems in standard aircraft restoration programs before completing his advanced Master Technician certifications in European and Japanese imports. Over the last decade, he has specialized in complex hybrid electric battery pack rebuilds, automated electronic clutches, and computer mapping recalibrations.

When he is not in the shop, Marcus hosts performance diagnostics workshops for regional automotive colleges and serves as a lead advisor on local environmental clean emission projects.
