Engine Diagnostics & Performance Calibration

Category: Core Engine Service
Estimated Duration: 1.5 - 3 Hours
Starting Price: From $89.00

At AutoCare, we utilize state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment to interface directly with your vehicle's Engine Control Unit (ECU). This allows our master certified technicians to decode fault codes, read live sensor streams, and accurately identify performance bottlenecks. We don't believe in guesswork; our precision scanning process isolates mechanical or electronic failures with 100% accuracy, saving you time and cost.

Whether you are experiencing a persistent "Check Engine" light, sudden power loss, rough idling, or reduced fuel efficiency, our digital diagnostics suite scans every sub-system. From fuel injectors and ignition coils to oxygen sensors and emissions systems, we ensure your vehicle is returned to optimal manufacturer specifications, providing a smoother ride and complete peace of mind on the road.
