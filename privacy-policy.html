01 / Parameters Logged

Information Collection

At AutoCare, we catalog and store essential user credentials and vehicle parameters to provide our high-fidelity garage diagnostics and repair scheduling operations. The parameters we collect fall into two primary classification profiles:

A. Active User Parameters

These consist of details you submit directly into our scheduling and feedback modules, including full legal name, digital electronic mail address, physical vehicle retrieval coordinates, active cellular coordinates, and vehicle chassis identification parameters (e.g., VIN codes, model fitment details).

B. Passive Mechanical Logs

When interfacing your machine with our computerized OBD-II telemetry receivers during garage diagnostic sessions, we catalogue mechanical performance parameters, fault codes, engine cylinder calibration readouts, brake wear metrics, and suspension alignment coordinates. This telemetry is stored to map your vehicle's structural performance history.

02 / Browser Cookies

Cookies & Tracker Nodes

Our digital portal deploys browser cookie variables, tracking nodes, and micro-session variables to personalize your interface experience and remember your spare part cart preferences. These cookies help us track site diagnostics, analyze user flow, and coordinate continuous load balancing.

The standard classifications of browser storage objects we implement include:

  • Core Session Cookies: Essential for processing appointment reservation states and maintaining active authentication statuses inside client portals.
  • Interface Personalization: Used to record user settings, typography preferences, and preferred mechanical branch locations.
  • Performance Analytics: Aggregates anonymized speed reports, network latency logs, and viewport width stats to help our engineers optimize layout performance.

You may adjust your browser's security console to refuse all cookies or notify you when a cookie variable is spawned. However, blocking core cookies will disable appointment scheduling and part checkout modules.

03 / Associated Partners

Third-party Services

To deliver modern automotive web features, we securely exchange verified parameters with verified third-party partners. These processing networks are strictly prohibited from utilizing your mechanical logs for any secondary commercial purposes:

  • Financial Payment Gateways: All direct parts checkouts and workshop invoice deposits are proxied through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment brokers (e.g. Stripe, PayPal) for credit card routing.
  • Cartographic Tracking Services: We embed interactive Google Maps components on our contact page to plot workshop entrance corridors. These widgets may log your physical GPS coordinates to plot route directions.
  • Emergency Towing Dispatch: Fleet tracking networks receive geographical coordinates to guide roadside emergency flatbeds to stalled vehicles.
04 / Encryption Protocols

Core Data Security

We treat client records with the same meticulous standards applied to engine block restorations. All active and passive data packets are safeguarded using cutting-edge commercial security technologies:

All database connections are sealed behind SSL/TLS transport-layer encryption, preventing packet interception. Our physical server cages are monitored 24/7, and administrative database panels require strict multi-factor authentication (MFA) credentials. We run daily diagnostic audits and automated database rollbacks to eliminate potential points of system failure.

05 / Mechanical Audit

User Rights & Auditing

As the legal owner of your personal parameters and mechanical records, you maintain full authority over their storage lifespan. Under commercial regulations, you possess the following active rights:

  • The Right to Inspect: You may request a complete, machine-readable CSV output of all diagnostic history, contact messages, and registration variables catalogued under your name.
  • The Right to Rectify: You can edit incorrect fitment parameters, email addresses, or phone numbers at any time.
  • The Right to Erase: You may file a data teardown ticket to purge your vehicle metrics, diagnostic files, and reservation lists from our live database nodes. Anonymized tax and accounting logs required for audit trails will remain preserved.
06 / Routine Calibration

Policy Calibration Updates

We routinely calibrate our security rules and parameter policies to align with new legal directives, database architectures, and diagnostic standards. Any revision of parameter rules will be signaled by updating the "Last Calibration" timestamp on the sidebar card.

We recommend clients periodically review this legal workspace to stay informed on how we isolate and shield mechanical diagnostics from unauthorized commercial scraping.
